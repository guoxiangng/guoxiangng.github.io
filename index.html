About

I design and operate complex cloud environments end to end — technical architecture, cloud security and networking, compliance, DevOps, containerization, and systems administration. I've led squads and cross-functional teams to deliver large AWS implementations for banks, government agencies, and defence, and I'm equally comfortable going deep hands-on or setting direction and mentoring engineers.

Right now I'm in a phase of rapid learning — using AI as a force-multiplier to explore new technologies quickly, in service of building platforms and enabling AI workloads. That's pulled me deep into the CNCF and open-source ecosystem: Kubernetes and OpenShift, GitOps with ArgoCD, KAgent, and observability with the Grafana and OpenTelemetry stacks, among many others. I've also hopped onto the vibe-coding bandwagon — building my own apps and agents for the fun of it.
